## Rules

- Do not infer height, nationality, preferred foot, match statistics, performance metrics, achievements, contract status, agent details or contact information.
- Keep the uploaded player photograph as the original club-supplied asset; do not replace it with generated or stock imagery.
- A shirt number observed in a photo should be treated as provisional until club staff confirm it.
- The profiles above concern minors. Before publishing identifiable profile photos, videos or personal information, obtain the club approvals and parent/guardian consent required by applicable law and safeguarding policy.
- Store a publication authorization status and reviewer/timestamp in the eventual database. Do not permit public publishing until required checks are satisfied.
- Keep unapproved profiles private and allow administrators to unpublish immediately.
- Do not treat this markdown table as the eventual database. The production system needs authenticated admin access, server-side validation, audit logs, protected media storage, and server-side access controls.
